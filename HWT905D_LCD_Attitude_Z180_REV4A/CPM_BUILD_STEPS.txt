HWTLCD4A CP/M Build Steps
=========================

1. Put HWTLCD4A.ASM on your CP/M drive.

2. Assemble:

   ASM HWTLCD4A

3. If assembly is clean, convert HEX to COM:

   LOAD HWTLCD4A

4. Run:

   HWTLCD4A

Good ASM output has no U/E/O errors. Example:

   06xx
   005H USE FACTOR
   END OF ASSEMBLY

The USE FACTOR line is normal. Do not worry about it.
