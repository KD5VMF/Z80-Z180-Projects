HWTCHS3 CP/M BUILD STEPS
========================

1. Copy HWTCHS3.ASM to your CP/M drive.

2. Assemble it:

   C>ASM HWTCHS3

3. Convert HEX to COM:

   C>LOAD HWTCHS3

4. Run it:

   C>HWTCHS3

Controls while running:

   Q  Quit back to CP/M
   +  Faster
   -  Slower

Notes:

- This is designed for CP/M ASM.COM style use.
- The source avoids OUT instructions and direct board hardware writes.
- Output is through BDOS console calls and ANSI escape sequences.
- A terminal with ANSI color support is recommended.
